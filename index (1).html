A doctor who explains things in plain words.

I trained in internal medicine and spent most of my career in community clinics, where good care starts with a proper conversation. I take time to ask about sleep, stress, food and family history, because they often explain more than a lab report.

Outside the clinic I teach first aid in schools and run free screening camps for blood pressure and diabetes.

How I can help

Check-ups and screening

A full review of your health: blood pressure, sugar, cholesterol, vaccines and age-based screening. You leave with a clear plan, not a pile of reports.

Long-term condition care

Ongoing support for diabetes, hypertension, thyroid and asthma, with regular follow-ups and simple goals you can track at home.

Family and child health

Growth checks, vaccinations and common illnesses for children, plus guidance for parents on nutrition and sleep.

Online consultations

Video or phone visits for follow-ups, prescription reviews and second opinions, so distance is never a reason to skip care.
